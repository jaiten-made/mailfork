# Security

MailFork is provided as-is and maintained on a best-effort basis. There is no support or response-time promise.

Anything that could link a Profile to its Owner or reveal the Personal Inbox counts as a vulnerability, for example a header or bounce that leaks the Personal Inbox address, or a Retired Profile Address accepting mail again.

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub: open the repo's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Please don't open a public issue for them.
